This example demonstrates how to acquire a scientific image from the Hera Asteroid Framing
Camera (AFC) and process it to reduce data volume before storage.
The core logic uses a Smart RLE (Run-Length Encoding) algorithm. RLE is a lossless compres-
sion method, meaning the reconstructed image is identical to the original. The algorithm works
line-by-line (1020 pixels at a time) compressing the line. If the compressed result is smaller than
the original it will save the compressed line, otherwise it will save the original raw line.
